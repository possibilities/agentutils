# 0002 — Bare fxnk Surface

The Surface renders the focused Document, an optional outlined Configuration picker, either one alone, or a quiet standby field. The picker's two controls occupy three bottom rows. An open option list and its control become one half-width flyover, joined by the dimmest divider role; it covers the Document without resizing it while the rest of the Surface sits under fxnk's 20% black modal backdrop. It borrows visual roles solely from the fxnk style guide and has no persistent header, footer, filename, path, line-number gutter, help text, send affordance, or surrounding controls.
